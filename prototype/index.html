Pilot version 5: what's new
  • New: Science Grades 3–6, Term 3. All 53 competencies from the official Budget of Work have ready worksheets (Foundation, Core, Extension) and a hands-on activity that is printed on the worksheet and used in the lesson plan. Weather and volcano items follow the current PAGASA wind signals (Signals 1–5) and PHIVOLCS alert levels.
  • English Grades 2–6, Term 3. All 300 competencies from the official Budget of Work are included. 214 have ready worksheets (word study, grammar, vocabulary, figures of speech, reading comprehension with original passages). 86 are speaking, writing or viewing tasks with task cards and a scoring rubric.
  • New: rate and fix any question. On the Worksheets tab, use 👍 (good), 👎 (needs work) or ⚠ (answer is wrong). Use ✏️ to edit the question and answer. Your feedback is saved and can be downloaded for the GuroPrep team.
  • Grades 1–6 Mathematics follow DepEd's official Three-Term Budget of Work. Numbers in math questions can be changed, and the answers update.
  • ILAW lesson plans (DepEd Order No. 016, s. 2026). PDF for short, long or A4 paper. Editable Word file. Answer key as a separate file.
