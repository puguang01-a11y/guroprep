Pilot version 3: what's new
  • New: Grades 1–6 Mathematics now follow DepEd's official Three-Term Budget of Work (SY 2026–2027). All competencies are listed by term and week. The current week is highlighted.
  • ✅ Ready competencies come with Foundation, Core and Extension worksheets, an exit ticket, and answers that are computed automatically. 📝 Template competencies need drawings or hands-on work, so you add your own items.
  • You can change any number in a question, and its answer updates. ↻ gives you a new question.
  • The lesson plan uses the ILAW format (DepEd Order No. 016, s. 2026). "Redo" changes only one part.
  • Other subjects and grades: you get an ILAW template and a link to DepEd's official Budget of Work for your grade.
  • PDF for short, long or A4 bond paper. Word (.docx) file. The answer key is a separate file. Prep time is measured automatically.
